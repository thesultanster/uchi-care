Privacy Policy

Sticky Chores is the customer-facing name of the Uchi app and website, operated by RNA Labs LLC. This policy explains the app and website separately because their marketing data practices are different.

The short version

  • The Sticky Chores app does not contain advertising or a Meta tracking SDK.
  • The website waitlist stores the email and optional answer you submit so we can send early-access and product updates.
  • The website records first-party campaign parameters so we can tell which pages and ads produce signups.
  • On the U.S.-focused waitlist, Meta Pixel and matching conversion events run unless you opt out or your browser sends Global Privacy Control.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Website measurement choices

We use first-party funnel events—such as a landing-page view, form start, signup, and confirmation—to understand whether the website works. These events use a random session identifier and campaign parameters, not the email stored in the marketing-events table.

On the U.S.-focused waitlist, Meta Pixel may load by default and set Meta measurement cookies. When you submit the form, the signup endpoint may send Meta a matching Lead conversion containing a one-way hash of your normalized email and technical matching data. The browser and server use the same event ID so Meta can deduplicate the conversion. You can turn Meta measurement off at any time through Privacy choices in the website footer. We also honor a browser Global Privacy Control signal. Opting out does not affect the website or your waitlist spot.

Service providers

  • Supabase: database, authentication, server functions, and file storage.
  • Resend: waitlist confirmation, product emails, audience status, and unsubscribe handling.
  • Meta: website ad measurement through Meta Pixel and Conversions API unless you opt out or use Global Privacy Control.
  • Google Gemini API: receives selected images only when you ask the app to generate artwork.
  • RevenueCat and Apple: subscription status, payments, refunds, and purchase management.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data or household content.
  • The Sticky Chores app does not display ads or include Meta Pixel.
  • We do not send chore names, household answers, passwords, or private household content to Meta.
  • We do not read contacts, location, or your photo library beyond the image you explicitly choose.

Your controls

  • Turn Meta ad measurement on or off through Privacy choices in the website footer.
  • Use a browser that sends Global Privacy Control to opt out automatically.
  • Confirm or ignore a waitlist request; an unconfirmed address is not added to the confirmed mailing audience.
  • Unsubscribe from product emails using the link in an email.
  • Delete your app account from Profile → Account → Delete account.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us.

Children

Sticky Chores is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

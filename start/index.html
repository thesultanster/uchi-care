What invisible labor actually is

Housework starts before anyone picks up a sponge.

Someone notices what needs attention, remembers what repeats, decides when and how it should happen, and keeps track of what “done” means. That thinking is work too.

Sticky Chores turns the whole job into a manga card both partners can see, understand, and own from beginning to end.

Chapter one · Make it visible

The mental list becomes a shared deck.

Kitchen. Laundry. Errands. Money. The work stops living in one person’s head and becomes something both partners can look at together.

  • ZoneWhere the work belongs
  • OwnerWho carries the whole job
  • RhythmWhen it comes back

Chapter two · Own the whole job

One card carries the thinking and the doing.

A chore is not only the visible final step. Every card keeps noticing, planning, execution, and the shared definition of done in one place.

Spot itThe mental load: noticing and remembering.

Sort itThe coordination: timing, supplies, and decisions.

Do itThe physical work: finishing the complete job.

Chapter three · Remember together

Stay ahead without keeping score.

Today, weekly, and monthly work stays visible. When a card is cleared, both partners share the same memory of what happened—without reminders, interrogations, or tallying points against each other.

“The review is a shared memory, not a verdict.”

The transformation

A calmer home is the real clear.

The manga makes chores satisfying. The structure makes the relationship lighter: less reminding, fewer recurring negotiations, and more room to simply be a couple.

Questions · Answered

Before you join.

Sticky Chores is an adult household system wrapped in a manga game—not a chore chart and not a relationship scoreboard.

Is Sticky Chores available now?

We’re preparing iPhone early access. Join the waitlist and confirm your email so we can invite you when the next cohort opens.

Does my partner need the app?

Sticky Chores is designed as a shared experience. One person starts the house, then their partner joins with a short invite code.

Does Review turn chores into a competition?

No. Review gives both partners the same memory of what was cleared. It is designed to reduce fuzzy recall and scorekeeping, not create a winner and loser.

What emails will I receive?

Only early-access invitations and meaningful product updates. Confirming your spot is optional, and every update includes an unsubscribe link.

Why couples, specifically

A co-op game, not a scoreboard.

Sticky Chores is for couples who love manga, games, and each other—but do not love having the same conversation about the sink. It makes invisible work visible without turning either person into the household manager.

Questions · Answered

Before you join the arc.

Is Sticky Chores available now?

We’re preparing iPhone early access. Join the waitlist and confirm your email so we can invite you when the next cohort opens.

Does my partner need the app?

Sticky Chores is designed as a shared experience. One person starts the house, then their partner joins with a short invite code.

Is this really for adults?

Yes. The manga makes chores more satisfying; the actual system is built around adult ownership, agreed standards, and shared history—not allowance points or gold stars.

What emails will I receive?

Only early-access invitations and meaningful product updates. Confirming your spot is optional, and every update includes an unsubscribe link.
